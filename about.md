My professional career started in early 2010, since then I have been involved in multiple enterprise solutions on a different levels: web, mobile, backend, background services and DB. I have been architecting and developing performance critical services and big data sync / migration tools. Developing e-commerce solutions, native mobile and cross-platform self service applications for well known mobile operators. Using SSIS to move enormous amount of data from MySQL to MSSQL within a very tight timeframe while converting and formatting the data. Building scalable cloud backend solutions and using Intune to distribute enterprise mobile solutions.

I am a former Microsoft MVP, recognised for being passionate about Xamarin and to be more specific Xamarin.Forms. I have been contributing by reporting bugs, developing Visual Studio extensions, sharing my experience with the community and collaborating in open source projects.

## Penetration testing

Cyber security, and offensive security in particular, is where most of my learning goes today. I practise penetration testing hands-on and rank in the top 10 all-time in Estonia on the [TryHackMe leaderboard](https://tryhackme.com/leaderboards).

Years of building web, mobile and backend systems shape how I approach it: I look at an application the way its developers do, which helps me find weaknesses and explain how to fix them at the root, in the code and in the design.

## Leadership

Engineering management is a passion of mine. I care about how teams work as much as about the software they build: helping people grow, keeping the work efficient and making sure the team succeeds together.

## Liamyte

I run [Liamyte OÜ](https://liamyte.com), a company based in Tartu, Estonia, offering software architecture, offensive security and product development. Under it I also publish my own [iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch apps](https://apps.liamyte.com).

I always welcome new ideas and am eager to learn.
